常见问题（FAQ）

有免费试用吗？

有。首次安装即可完整体验全部功能 7 天，无需注册、无需账号。试用结束后，可购买解锁完整驾驶体验（支持一次性解锁或月度订阅）。

如何连接我的 Tesla 车辆？

请先确认车辆与 iPhone 距离足够近，然后在 Tesla 车机与手机端开启蓝牙。首次连接时，请在 Tesla 车辆上授权蓝牙配对，并在 iPhone 上允许 Tesla Dash 使用蓝牙权限。

蓝牙连接不上 / 数据不显示？

① 确认 iPhone「设置 → 蓝牙」已开启；② 确认 Tesla 车辆已唤醒；③ 在 iPhone「设置 → Tesla Dash」中允许蓝牙权限；④ 尝试关闭再重新打开应用，或在车辆端重新配对。

点击解锁提示 “Unable to load product”？

请确认 iPhone 已登录 App Store 账号且网络连接正常。若仍无法加载，请尝试点击「恢复购买」，或稍后重试。问题持续存在时，请通过上方邮箱联系我们。

如何恢复已购买的内容？

请在应用内设置页点击「恢复购买」，并使用购买时的 Apple ID 登录。恢复不会重复扣费。

车速显示偶尔延迟或不稳定？

这是蓝牙数据短暂延迟时的正常表现。应用会使用 iPhone 定位辅助稳定速度显示，请在 iPhone「设置 → 隐私 → 定位服务」中允许 Tesla Dash 使用定位。

我的数据会被上传吗？

不会。所有车辆数据仅在设备本地处理与显示，不上传至任何外部服务器。详见隐私政策。

FAQ

Is there a free trial?

Yes. New users can experience all features free for 7 days — no registration and no account required. After the trial, unlock the full driving experience with a one-time purchase or a monthly subscription.

How do I connect to my Tesla?

Keep your iPhone near the vehicle and enable Bluetooth on both the vehicle and your iPhone. On first connection, authorize Bluetooth pairing on the Tesla and allow Bluetooth permission for Tesla Dash on your iPhone.

Bluetooth won't connect / no data shown?

① Confirm Bluetooth is on in iPhone Settings; ② Confirm the vehicle is awake; ③ Allow Bluetooth for Tesla Dash in iPhone Settings; ④ Quit and reopen the app, or re-pair from the vehicle.

“Unable to load product” when tapping unlock?

Confirm your iPhone is signed in to the App Store and has a working network connection. If it still fails, try “Restore Purchases” or retry later. If the issue persists, contact us at the email above.

How do I restore my purchases?

Tap “Restore Purchases” in the in-app settings and sign in with the Apple ID used for the purchase. Restoring never charges you again.

Speed display occasionally lags or is unstable?

This is normal during brief Bluetooth latency. The app uses iPhone location services to help stabilize the speed display. Allow location access for Tesla Dash in iPhone Settings → Privacy → Location Services.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. All vehicle data is processed and displayed locally on your device and is not uploaded to any external server. See the Privacy Policy for details.
