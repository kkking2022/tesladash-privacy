1. 概述

Tesla Dash（"本应用"）由独立开发者制作，是一款用于显示 Tesla 车辆信息的 iOS 应用。本应用与 Tesla, Inc. 无任何关联，非官方应用。Tesla 是 Tesla, Inc. 的注册商标。

2. 数据收集与使用

本应用不收集、不上传、不分享任何用户数据。

本应用通过蓝牙（BLE）直接连接 Tesla 车辆，读取的数据（如车速、电量、续航等）仅在本设备本地处理并显示，不会传输到任何外部服务器。

3. 数据处理与本地存储

Tesla Dash 的车辆数据仅在设备本地实时处理。车辆显示数据（速度、电量、续航等）不会被持久化保存。用户设置（主题选择、单位偏好、亮度等）保存在设备本地。

4. 蓝牙权限

本应用需要蓝牙权限以连接您的 Tesla 车辆。蓝牙数据仅用于本地显示，不会用于其他目的。

5. 定位权限

Tesla Dash 会使用 iPhone 的定位能力获取设备当前速度，用于在蓝牙车辆速度数据短暂延迟或中断时辅助稳定仪表盘速度显示。定位数据仅在设备本地实时处理，不会上传至服务器，也不会被持久化保存。

6. 第三方服务

本应用不包含任何第三方追踪、分析或广告 SDK。

7. 免责声明

Effective Date: August 10, 2026

1. Overview

Tesla Dash ("this App") is developed by an independent developer. It is an iOS application for displaying Tesla vehicle information. This App is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Tesla, Inc. Tesla is a registered trademark of Tesla, Inc.

2. Data Collection and Use

This App does not collect, upload, or share any user data.

This App connects directly to your Tesla vehicle via Bluetooth (BLE). Data read from the vehicle (such as speed, battery level, range, etc.) is processed and displayed locally on your device only, and is not transmitted to any external servers.

3. Data Processing and Local Storage

Tesla Dash processes vehicle data locally on your device in real time. Vehicle display data (speed, battery, range, etc.) is not persistently stored. User settings (theme selection, unit preferences, brightness, etc.) are saved locally on the device.

4. Bluetooth Permission

This App requires Bluetooth permission to connect to your Tesla vehicle. Bluetooth data is used for local display only and will not be used for any other purpose.

5. Location Permission

Tesla Dash uses the iPhone's location services to obtain the device's current speed. This information is used only to help stabilize the dashboard speed display when Bluetooth vehicle-speed data is temporarily delayed or unavailable. Location data is processed locally in real time and is not uploaded to any server or persistently stored.

6. Third-Party Services

This App does not contain any third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs.
